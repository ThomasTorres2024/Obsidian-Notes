# Law of Noncontradiction
For any [[Proposition]] $P$, it cannot be the case that $P$ and its proposition are simultaneously true, which means that the following is a contradiction:
$$P \wedge \neg P $$
And that the two claims are mutually exclusive. Formally we express this law as a tautology, namely as the following:
$$T \equiv \neg (P \wedge \neg P)$$
This fact helps enforce the [[Principle of Explosion]].
