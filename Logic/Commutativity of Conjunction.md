# Commutativity of Conjunction
[[Commutativity of Conjunction]] refers to the fact that for a set of [[Proposition]]s $P=\{ P_{1},P_{2},\cdots,P_{n} \}$ the following is equivalent regardless of the ordering of the propositions:
$$\bigwedge_{i=1}^{n} P_i $$
