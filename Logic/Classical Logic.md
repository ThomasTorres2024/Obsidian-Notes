# Classical Logic
Every logical system that is deemed classical shares the following properties:

1. The [[Law of Excluded Middle]], and [[Double Negation Elimination]]
2. The [[Law of Noncontradiction]] and the [[Principle of Explosion]]
3. [[Monotonicity of Entailment]] and [[Idempotency of Entailment]] 
4. [[Commutativity of Conjunction]] 
5. [[De Morgan Duality]]
