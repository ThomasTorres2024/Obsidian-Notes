# Skew-Symmetric Matrix 
A [[Skew-Symmetric Matrix]] is a [[Matrix]] where for $A \in \mathbb{C}^{n \times n}$ we have that $A=-A^H$ where $H$ is the [[Hermitian]] operator. Notably, skew symmetric matrices are [[Normal Matrices]] and thus can be unitarily diagonalized. They also have purely imaginary [[Eigen Value]]s. 

In some sense the [[Skew-Symmetric Matrix]] and an associated [[Hermitian]] matrix are analogous to the [[Complex Numbers]].
