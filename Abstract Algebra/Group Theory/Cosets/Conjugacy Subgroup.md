# Conjugate Subgroup 
Given some [[Group]] $G$ and some [[Subgroup]] of $G$, $H$ such that $H \leq G$ then the Conjugate Subgroup of $H$ by a fixed $g \in G$ is given by:
$$gHg^{-1} = \{ ghg^{-1} : h \in H \}$$
If $H$ is normal then it follows that $H$ is a [[Normal Subgroup]] $\forall g \in G$.
